# Identity Theft Response Policy

Suspected Identity Theft: If a customer reports an account or credit line they did not open, initiate an identity theft case immediately. This includes placing a credit freeze with the bank's internal credit systems and advising the customer to file a report with the relevant national fraud reporting body.

Required Actions: Close or freeze the unauthorized account, document all affected products, and provide the customer with a case reference number and a checklist for reporting to external credit bureaus.

Timeline: Identity theft cases are treated as Critical priority and must be opened within 1 business hour of the report.
