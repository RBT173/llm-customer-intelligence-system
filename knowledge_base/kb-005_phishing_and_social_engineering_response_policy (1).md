# Fraud & Unauthorized Transaction Policy

Unrecognized Charges: Any charge a customer does not recognize should be verified against merchant descriptor data before escalation. Charges over $100 that remain unrecognized after descriptor lookup are escalated to the Fraud Team within 1 business hour.

Unauthorized Transactions: If a customer confirms they did not make a transaction, the card must be frozen immediately, pending charges reversed provisionally, and a fraud case opened. Provisional credit is issued within 10 business days per standard dispute timelines.

Lost or Stolen Cards: Must be blocked immediately upon report, regardless of time of day, with a replacement card issued within 5-7 business days (expedited shipping available).
