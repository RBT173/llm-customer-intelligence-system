# Foreign Transaction & FX Policy

Exchange Rate Markup: International purchases are converted at the interbank rate plus a 2.5% foreign transaction fee. This markup is disclosed in the account terms and is standard across all consumer card products.

International Card Usage: All debit and credit cards work internationally by default unless a security block is active. Customers traveling abroad should set a travel notice to prevent fraud-related declines, but a travel notice is not required for the card to function internationally.

Travel Notices: Can be set for up to 90 days in advance and cover a specified date range and list of countries.
