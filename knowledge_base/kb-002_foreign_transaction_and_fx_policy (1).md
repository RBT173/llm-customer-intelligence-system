# Billing & Fees Policy

Overdraft Fees: A $35 overdraft fee is charged only when an account balance goes below $0 after a transaction posts. Fees applied when the balance never went negative are billed in error and must be reversed upon verification of the ledger.

Monthly Maintenance Fee: Waived if the account maintains a minimum daily balance of $1,500, or if the customer has $500+ in monthly direct deposits.

Late Fees & Autopay: If autopay is active and the payment fails due to a system error (not insufficient funds), any late fee charged must be reversed automatically. Customers should be asked to confirm the autopay enrollment date to cross-check against the payment log.

Cancellation Fees: Subscription products may carry a cancellation fee only if canceled within the first 90 days of enrollment. After 90 days, cancellation is free of charge.
