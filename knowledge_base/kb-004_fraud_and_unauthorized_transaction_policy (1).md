# Interest & Credit Policy

Interest & Grace Period: Interest is not charged on purchases if the full statement balance is paid by the due date shown on the most recent statement. If a payment posts after the due date -- even by one day -- interest accrues from the transaction date, not the due date. Payments must be verified against the exact statement cutoff time.

Credit Limit Increases: Customers are eligible for a credit limit increase if the account is at least 6 months old, has no late payments in the last 12 months, and current utilization is below 50%.

Premium Tier Upgrade: Requires an average balance of $10,000+ over the trailing 3 months or $2,000+ in monthly qualifying direct deposits. Premium tier waives most standard fees.
