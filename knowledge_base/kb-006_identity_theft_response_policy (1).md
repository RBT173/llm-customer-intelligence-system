# Phishing & Social Engineering Response Policy

Identifying Phishing: The bank never asks customers to confirm a PIN, full card number, or online banking password via text, email, or unsolicited phone call. Any message requesting this information should be treated as fraudulent.

Customer Guidance: Advise customers not to click links or respond to suspicious messages, to report the message to the Fraud Team, and to monitor their account for the next 30 days. If any information was shared, the account should be flagged for enhanced monitoring and credentials reset immediately.

Vishing (Phone Scams): Same guidance applies. Confirm with the customer whether any sensitive data was disclosed before deciding whether to lock the account.
