# Account Services Policy

Joint Account Requests: Both parties must be present (in person or via verified video call) and provide government-issued ID. Existing account history transfers to the joint account; a new agreement must be signed by both holders.

Name Changes: Require legal documentation (marriage certificate, court order, or updated government ID) before the name is updated on the account and reissued cards.

Account Closure: Accounts may be closed once all pending transactions clear and the balance is zero or withdrawn. Closure requests are processed within 1-2 business days.
